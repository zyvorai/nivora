Installing Nivora

Requirements: macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon.

Production use (INR 999 incl. GST in India; about US$10 excl. tax elsewhere). After payment you receive an email with a GST invoice and a download link. Verify the download against the .sha256 shown on the payment page or sent on request. Licences are bought at https://zyvor.dev/buy#nivora. Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Custom pricing: contact sales@zyvor.dev.

Install#

  1. Download Nivora-X.Y.Z.dmg and Nivora-X.Y.Z.dmg.sha256 from Releases.
  2. Verify the download: shasum -a 256 Nivora-X.Y.Z.dmg must print the value in the .sha256 file.
  3. Open the DMG and drag Nivora to Applications.
  4. Open Nivora. The build is signed with our Apple Developer ID and notarized, so Gatekeeper opens it normally, without a warning.

Closing the window keeps Nivora in the menu bar; Quit is in the menu-bar menu. A welcome sheet appears once on first launch.

Trial key and licence#

Free 30-day trial key: sales@zyvor.dev. Paste the key under Nivora > Licence…; the sidebar then shows your licence status. Nothing is disabled without a key. To buy a licence, go to https://zyvor.dev/buy. Earlier paid purchases keep their perpetual licence and need no key.

What the notarized build can and cannot do#

WorksNot in the notarized build
Per-app flows and byte counters through the built-in collectorContent-filter system extension (per-flow app attribution from the system)
Interfaces, DNS, routes, VPNs, neighbours, listenersCapture helper (root tcpdump, approved in System Settings)
Time Machine history, health, exports, filters, menu bar, widgetBlocking rules taking effect

The content filter and the capture helper need an Apple NetworkExtension entitlement that is not available to us, so per-app attribution uses the collector. Nivora runs in observe-first mode.

Uninstall#

pkill -x Nivora
rm -rf /Applications/Nivora.app
rm -rf ~/.local/share/nivora          # history; optional
