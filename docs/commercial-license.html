Nivora Production-Use License (draft for review by Zyvor AI Labs Private Limited and its counsel)

Nivora is source-available under the Zyvor Production License v1.0 (LICENSE). Non-production use is free. Under section 4 of that licence, Production Use needs a separate written commercial license from Zyvor AI Labs Private Limited. This page describes the one-time paid grant that is that commercial license for Nivora, covering production use, including business and commercial use.

Free use#

Anyone may use Nivora for Non-Production Use (as defined in LICENSE) without paying: download the app from the GitHub release and use it for non-production purposes. Use that supports live business operations, employees, customers or revenue is Production Use and needs the paid grant.

The paid grant#

On receipt of payment for a Nivora build (₹999 including GST, about US$12, per build), Zyvor AI Labs Private Limited ("Licensor") grants the payer ("Licensee", an individual or a single legal entity) a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to:

  1. use Nivora for Production Use, including business and commercial purposes, on machines the Licensee owns or operates;
  2. do so for the version bought and every later version released by Licensor under the Zyvor Production License v1.0 or a successor Zyvor licence;
  3. modify the source for the Licensee's own use.

What it does not cover#

  • Redistribution, sublicensing, reselling, or hosting Nivora as a product or service for third parties.
  • Redistribution rights beyond section 8 of LICENSE, which requires prior written permission.
  • Removing licence notices (LICENSE, SPDX headers).
  • More than one legal entity: affiliates and customers need their own grant.
  • A warranty that the developer build's content filter or capture helper can be activated: they need Apple entitlements and a provisioning profile that the Licensee must obtain, and they are compile-verified only.

Proof and terms#

  • The invoice or payment receipt is the proof of a paid grant. Earlier paid grants are perpetual and need no key.
  • From 0.5.4 the app can read an optional licence key under Nivora > Licence…, for example the free 30-day trial key from sales@zyvor.dev; licences are obtained at https://zyvor.dev/buy. Nivora keeps working in every state: a missing, trial or expired key never disables a feature, and nothing is sent to us.
  • Provided as is, no warranty, under the same disclaimers as the Zyvor Production License v1.0. Payments are non-refundable except as required by law.
  • Questions, receipts, new download links, and larger or multi-entity licences: sales@zyvor.dev.

Open questions for counsel#

  • Whether ₹999 is intended to cover large organisations or should be capped by entity size.
  • The exact line between Non-Production and Production Use for individuals running Nivora on their own Mac, and for freelancers and employees using it at work.
